Terms & Privacy Policy

Terms of Service

By using Gazellian, you agree to the following terms and conditions. Failure to comply may result in account suspension or termination.

1. User Responsibilities

  • Users must provide accurate and truthful information when registering.
  • Any fraudulent activities, including fake bookings or misleading service listings, are strictly prohibited.
  • Users must not engage in abusive or harmful behavior towards others on the platform.

2. Service Provider Obligations

  • Service providers must ensure that their listings accurately represent their services.
  • Providers must fulfill all accepted bookings unless exceptional circumstances arise.
  • Repeated cancellations or poor service may result in suspension or removal from the platform.

3. Bookings & Payments

  • All bookings must be made through the Gazellian platform to ensure security and accountability.
  • Payments are processed through a secure gateway; Gazellian does not store sensitive financial data.
  • Refunds and disputes must be handled in accordance with Gazellian’s refund policy.

4. Dispute Resolution

  • Any disputes between users and service providers should first be resolved through communication.
  • If a resolution cannot be reached, users may escalate disputes to Gazellian’s support team.
  • Gazellian reserves the right to make final decisions on unresolved disputes.

Your privacy is important to us. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

  • We collect personal details such as name, email, and phone number for account creation and communication.
  • Activity data, such as bookings and interactions, may be stored for analytics and support purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To facilitate service bookings and provider recommendations.
  • To provide customer support and resolve issues.
  • To improve platform functionality based on user behavior and feedback.

3. Data Protection

  • We use encryption and secure servers to protect your personal data.
  • Only authorized personnel have access to sensitive user information.

4. Third-Party Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
  • Your information may be shared with service providers only as necessary for bookings.

6. User Rights

  • Users may update or delete their accounts, subject to our retention policies.
